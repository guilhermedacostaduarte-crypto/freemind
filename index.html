The H.A.L.T. Diagnostic

To disarm the cycle, you must intercept the trigger. The H.A.L.T. technique helps you identify biological discomforts that your brain misinterprets as an "urge to use your phone":

Hungry: Without glucose, your prefrontal cortex (self-control) shuts down.
Angry/Anxious: You use the screen as a pacifier for negative emotions.
Lonely: The internet simulates the social connection you lack.
Tired: Willpower is a battery that drains by nightfall. That's when 90% of relapses happen.
